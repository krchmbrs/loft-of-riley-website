The Home

Your private corner
of the Oregon Coast.

Step off the deck and you're on the sand. Literally. No road to cross, no path to find — just the Pacific, stretching out in front of you, yours for the weekend.

Inside, the Loft of Riley is warm, thoughtfully designed, and spacious enough for the whole crew. Four bedrooms, a chef-stocked kitchen, a game room loft for the kids, radiant-heated bathroom floors, and the kind of open living room where nobody wants to leave — because the view from the picture windows is simply that good.

Kris has thought of everything, from the KitchenAid mixer and Japanese knives to the pebble ice maker, the karaoke machine, and firewood already stacked on the deck. You just show up.

Location

Arch Cape —
the Oregon Coast's best kept secret.

Tucked between Cannon Beach to the north and Manzanita to the south, Arch Cape is the quieter, more private stretch of coast that locals keep to themselves. The beach in front of the Loft of Riley feels practically private — especially compared to the crowds in town.

But "secluded" doesn't mean isolated. Everything is close.

  • Steps away Private beach access — right from the backyard
  • 9 min walk Oswald West State Park old-growth trails
  • 12 min walk Falcon Cove
  • 3 min drive Hug Point State Park & tide pools
  • 8 min drive Cannon Beach — shops, restaurants, Haystack Rock
  • 20 min drive Manzanita — great dining & a different vibe
  • ~90 min drive Portland
